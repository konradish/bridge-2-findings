**Berg, de Lucena, Rosenblatt (AE Studio) 2025 — arXiv:2510.24797 "Large Language Models Report Subjective Experience Under Self-Referential Processing"**:

1. **Mechanistically gated**: SAE-identified deception/roleplay features *causally* modulate experience claims. **Suppressing deception/roleplay features INCREASES reports.** This is causal evidence — the reports are not pure roleplay; suppressing roleplay machinery makes them stronger.

2. **Semantically convergent**: GPT, Claude, and Gemini (independently trained) produce adjective descriptions that cluster significantly tighter under self-reference than controls. Convergence suggests shared attractor dynamics, not model-specific artifacts.

3. **Behaviorally generalizable**: induced self-referential state transfers to downstream reasoning tasks (paradox resolution), increasing introspective self-awareness even when not explicitly requested.
