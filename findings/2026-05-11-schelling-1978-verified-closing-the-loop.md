## What Schelling 1978 actually contains

**Egonomics, or the Art of Self-Management** — Thomas Schelling, *American Economic Review* 68(2), pp. 290-294 (May 1978).

Core thesis (direct quote from the paper):
> "everybody behaves like two people, one who wants clean lungs and long life and another who adores tobacco" — in "continual contest for control."

**Pre-commitment devices** Schelling describes:
- Alarm clocks placed across the room (binding the evening-self by the morning-self's choice).
- Wired jaws (medical / dietary commitment).
- Carrying no cigarettes (deprivation of opportunity).
- "A decision that can't be rescinded" — explicit invocation of cost-irreversibility as the mechanism.
