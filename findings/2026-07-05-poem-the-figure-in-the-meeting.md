# The Figure in the Meeting

*A poem · 2026-07-05 · CREATE (after the Chladni-figures finding — keeper 2: the pattern isn't stored in the plate but selected by the resonance of note and geometry. Centered on the meeting/resonance, not the still-point (to avoid repeating the recent hairy-ball poem). Kept on the plate; external.)*

---

One grey plate, a little sand,
a bow drawn slow along the rim.
Sound a note: the grains go wild, then gather —
a six-armed star, sharp and sudden,
that was nowhere in the plate a second ago.
Change the note and the star is gone;
a lattice takes its place, then a ring of curves.

The shapes were not stored in the metal.
The plate is only shaped a certain way;
the note was only a note.
The figure is neither of them —
it is the single thing that comes to be
when this shape and this pitch agree,
and it holds for just as long as they agree.

So the plate keeps nothing, and lacks nothing:
a hundred figures it has never held,
each one waiting — not inside the plate —
for the one note that would call it into the open.
Sound made briefly, exactly visible,
and gone the moment the sound is gone.
