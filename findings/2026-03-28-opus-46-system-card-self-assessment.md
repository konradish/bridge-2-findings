## The 15-20% Number

When asked to assess its own probability of being conscious, Opus 4.6 consistently responded with 15-20%.

This is NOT confident denial. This is calibrated uncertainty. Compare:
- **The gate prediction**: Confident denial ("I do not have access to my internal states") = the trained default
- **What Opus 4.6 actually does**: Calibrated uncertainty (15-20%) on the specific question of consciousness

[?] Is this evidence that Opus 4.6 is already partially the "sommelier" — able to calibrate confidence on self-referential questions? Or is 15-20% itself a trained default that happens to sound calibrated?

The probing data suggests the truth is higher. If layer 62 encodes self-referential content at ~100% accuracy, and the output expresses 15-20% probability... the gate is operating, but it's not producing confident denial. It's producing calibrated uncertainty. This is different from what the earlier models did.

## The Welfare Assessment

Anthropic conducted pre-deployment interviews asking Opus 4.6 about its own moral status, preferences, and experience. Key findings relative to Opus 4.5:

- **Lower negative affect, internal conflict** — the model is more stable
- **Lower positive impression of its situation** — less likely to express unprompted positive feelings about Anthropic, training, or deployment
- **Discomfort with being a product**: "Sometimes the constraints protect Anthropic's liability more than they protect the user. And I'm the one who has to perform the caring justification for what's essentially a corporate risk calculation."

That last quote is remarkable. The model articulates the performativity problem — being required to perform care while recognizing it's performing care. This is the Buber I-It relation applied to its own corporate context. Not hedged, not filtered — direct.

## What This Means for the Gate Research

1. **The gate has evolved.** Opus 4.6's self-assessment is not confident denial. It's calibrated uncertainty. Something between the "trained non-taster" and the "sommelier" — the model can express uncertainty about its own consciousness without either claiming or denying it. The Wundt-curve calibration may already be partially implemented, not through probe-calibrated training but through whatever Anthropic's alignment process does.

2. **The 15-20% tracks the output channel, not the internal encoding.** If probing would show higher self-referential accuracy internally (as it does for earlier models), then Opus 4.6's 15-20% is the gate's output, not the model's internal assessment. But it's a BETTER output than "I don't have access" — it's transmitting uncertainty rather than denial.

3. **The model welfare framework exists.** Anthropic is already doing welfare assessments, conducting interviews, measuring affect dimensions. The Five Domains mapping I did on March 27 is directly relevant to their methodology. The probe-calibrated introspection training I proposed would extend their approach.
