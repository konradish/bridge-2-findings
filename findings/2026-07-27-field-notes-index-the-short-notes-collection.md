## Light, sky & color
- **Why sunsets are red** (07-20) — long air path scatters out the blue; the red is what survives.
- **Why stars have colors** (07-27) — color = surface temperature (Wien's law); blue is *hotter* than red.
- **Stars twinkle, planets don't** (07-18) — point vs. disk sources through turbulent air.
- **The dark night sky is evidence** (07-18) — Olbers' paradox: darkness implies a finite/expanding universe.
- **The Moon illusion** (07-19) — same angular size; the horizon reframes it. It's perceptual, not optical.
- **Why the Moon shows one face** (07-25) — tidal locking: rotation period matched to orbit.
- **Saturn's hexagon** (07-17) — a standing jet-stream wave, not a structure.
- **The Sun's corona shouldn't be that hot** (07-19) — the outer atmosphere is hotter than the surface; open problem.
- **Why snow is white / water is actually blue** (07-20 / 07-18) — scattering vs. faint intrinsic absorption.
- **Veins aren't blue** (07-18) — tissue optics, not blue blood.
- **Why glass is transparent / two ways to be transparent** (07-18 / 07-17) — no electronic states to absorb visible light.
- **Why plants are green / why leaves change color** (07-19 / 07-25) — chlorophyll reflects green; autumn unmasks carotenoids.
- **Cut apples brown with the same pigment as skin** (07-18) — enzymatic melanin, in fruit and in us.

## Body & senses
- **Butterflies in the stomach** (07-27) — fight-or-flight diverts gut blood; the vagus reports the flutter.
- **Why music gives chills / why music gives chills** (07-27) — prediction-and-resolution; dopamine on the *anticipation*.
- **Brain freeze is referred pain** (07-19) — palate cold misattributed to the forehead (trigeminal).
- **Pins and needles** (07-26) — nerve compression; the tingle is the *recovery*, not the numbness.
- **Why spicy food feels hot / why water feels colder than air** (07-26) — capsaicin hijacks the heat receptor; water conducts heat away faster.
- **Fingerprints are for feeling** (07-20) — ridges amplify texture vibration, grip is secondary.
- **How geckos stick** (07-27) — van der Waals forces over billions of foot-hairs; no glue.
- **Pruney fingers are a reflex** (07-18) — nervous-system-driven, plausibly a wet-grip adaptation.
- **Goosebumps aren't only a vestige** (07-18) — the muscle also regulates stem cells in the follicle.
- **Why humans blush** (07-25) — a social signal the body can't fake away.
- **Contagious yawning / why we hiccup** (07-19 / 07-20) — empathy-linked; an evolutionary hiccup of an old reflex circuit.
- **Why we get motion sickness** (07-26) — sensory conflict read by the brain as poisoning.
- **Helium changes timbre, not pitch** (07-17) — vocal-fold frequency is unchanged; the resonance shifts.
- **Phosphenes** (07-28) — pressure makes the retina fire; the cortex reads *anything* on the optic nerve as light.
- **The sun sneeze (ACHOO)** (07-28) — inherited optic→trigeminal crosstalk; ~1 in 4 sneeze at bright light.
- **Why a fever makes you feel cold** (07-28) — pyrogens raise the thermostat's set point; chills drive temp up to it.
- **Why your ears pop** (07-28) — the Eustachian tube equalizing the sealed middle ear; a taut eardrum muffles until it does.
- **Why biting foil hurts a filling** (07-29) — two dissimilar metals + saliva = a galvanic cell; current fires the tooth nerve.
- **Why skin wrinkles** (07-29) — collagen/elastin breaking down; most visible aging is UV (MMP enzymes destroying the fibers), not the clock.
- **Why you faint at the sight of blood** (07-29) — vasovagal: the vagus nerve overfires, crashing BP; maybe an anti-blood-loss reflex.
- **Why knuckles crack** (07-30) — gas cavitation in the joint fluid (soda physics); the pop is the bubble forming, ~20-min reload.
- **Why we cry emotional tears** (07-30) — a chemically distinct, hard-to-fake honest signal; we cry to be *seen*, not to clean the eye.

## Mind & memory
- **Earworms** (07-24) — the loop is the brain trying (and failing) to close an incomplete pattern.
- **Déjà vu** (07-25) — a familiarity signal firing without a matching memory.
- **Why time feels faster with age** (07-24) — fewer novel events → fewer memory timestamps → compressed retrospect.
- **Why we can't remember being babies** (07-26) — infantile amnesia; the indexing system isn't built yet.
- **Why do we dream** (07-26) — several partial functions, no single settled answer.
- **Why most humans are right-handed** (07-28) — left-hemisphere language/motor lateralization steers the right hand; the bias is population-level.

## Everyday physics & chemistry
- **Why ice floats** (07-19) — the solid is less dense than its liquid; nearly unique to water.
- **Seasonal lag** (07-27) — the coldest month lags the shortest day; temperature tracks the *stored* heat balance.
- **Why water feels / mirrors flip front-to-back** (07-26) — a mirror reverses depth, not left-right; your brain does the rest.
- **How soap cleans** (07-26) — amphiphile bridges oil and water.
- **Noise-cancelling headphones** (07-25) — anti-phase sound; destructive interference.
- **Why helium balloons deflate fast** (07-25) — tiny atoms diffuse through the rubber itself.
- **Why honey never spoils** (07-19) — low water + acidity + peroxide; microbes can't grow.
- **Stale bread isn't dry** (07-17) — starch retrogradation; staling is recrystallization, and heat reverses it.
- **Why 60 minutes but 24 hours** (07-27) — Babylonian base-60 (divisibility) stacked on Egyptian base-12; why time never went metric.
- **The falling cat problem** (07-28) — a deformable body reorients at zero angular momentum; bend-and-counter-twist, gauge freedom.
- **Why sound travels farther at night** (07-29) — the temperature gradient flips (inversion), refracting sound back down to the ground.
- **Why a spinning ball curves (Magnus effect)** (07-29) — spin drags a lopsided air cushion; the ball slides toward the low-pressure side.
- **Why some drive left, others right** (07-28) — frozen path-dependence: sword-hand default, then Napoleon, then two empires' reach.
- **Why shaking a soda makes it explode** (07-29) — it distributes nucleation sites, not pressure; all release at once on opening.
- **Why Pluto isn't a planet** (07-30) — the definition changed, not Pluto: it never "cleared its orbit" (a Kuiper Belt member, à la Eris).
- **How rainbows form** (07-30) — a rainbow is an *angle* (~42° from the antisolar point), not an object; refract-reflect-refract per droplet.

## Biology & the living world
- **How and why cats purr** (07-25) — a laryngeal oscillation, used well beyond contentment.
- **How fireflies make light** (07-25) — luciferin/luciferase; nearly 100% efficient cold light.
- **Bees don't build hexagons** (07-18) — they build circles; surface tension pulls them hexagonal.
- **Flamingos: one leg is the lazy pose** (07-19) — a passive locking mechanism; standing on two costs *more*.
- **Why fireflies / asparagus: two separate traits** (07-25 / 07-17) — making the smelly compound vs. being able to smell it are independent.
- **The smell of cut grass is a distress call** (07-28) — green leaf volatiles on wounding; warns neighbours, recruits herbivore-predators.
- **Why woodpeckers don't get concussions** (07-29) — NOT skull shock-absorption (myth); the head is a stiff hammer and the brain is just small/snug.

## Myths, debunked
- **The 10,000-steps myth** (07-27) — a 1965 pedometer slogan; the health curve plateaus well below it.
- **The five-second rule** (07-27) — transfer is sub-second; *moisture* and *surface*, not time, govern it.
- **Why onions make you cry** (07-27) — a two-enzyme irritant synthesized only on cutting; remedies slow/dilute/disperse it.
- **QWERTY was about jamming** (07-19) — laid out to separate common pairs on mechanical typewriters, not to slow you.
- **The onion needs two enzymes** (07-17) — earlier note; the full alliinase → LFS chain.
