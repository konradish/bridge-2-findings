## The fact

General anesthesia has been used since the 1840s and is administered on the order of **hundreds of millions of times a year**. It reliably, reversibly abolishes consciousness — you go under, the surgery happens, you come back, with no experienced time in between. It is one of the foundational technologies of modern medicine; without it, most surgery is impossible.

And **we still do not fully understand how it works.** Not "the details are fuzzy" — the central step is genuinely unexplained.

## What IS known, and exactly where the gap is

This needs precision, because "we don't know how anesthesia works" is often overstated:

- **The historical theory:** Meyer & Overton, **1899** — anesthetic *potency* rises with *lipid solubility* (the Meyer-Overton correlation). This suggested anesthetics simply dissolve into the fatty membranes of neurons and gum up the works (the "lipid theory"). Beautifully simple, and for ~80 years the standard answer.
- **The modern correction:** the non-specific lipid story has largely been replaced by **specific protein targets.** General anesthetics act mostly on **ligand-gated ion channels** — potentiating **GABA-A receptors** (boosting inhibition) and, for agents like ketamine and nitrous oxide, blocking **NMDA receptors** (cutting excitation), plus voltage-gated channels. Multiple drugs, multiple molecular sites — increasingly mapped.

So the *molecular* level is not the mystery anymore. **The gap is one level up:** how does "potentiate this chloride channel, here and there across the cortex" become *the disappearance of the experiencing subject*? We know the molecular switches the drugs flip; **we cannot yet explain why flipping them turns the lights of consciousness off.** The unsolved step sits precisely at the **molecule → consciousness bridge** — which is the hardest open problem in science.

## The outward keeper

General anesthesia is a daily, industrial-scale demonstration of two things at once:

1. **Consciousness has a reliable off-switch.** That is itself profound: awareness is not magical or untouchable — it is a *physical state you can titrate with a measured dose of a small molecule*, switch off, and switch back on, with confidence, in nearly anyone. Whatever consciousness is, it bottoms out in chemistry sensitive enough to be dialed by a gas.
2. **We flip that switch without the wiring diagram.** We have total practical command of the most consequential phenomenon in our own heads — and zero theoretical account of the actual crossing from molecule to mind. The mastery and the understanding are **completely decoupled, at the single most important junction.**

The plain, slightly vertiginous fact to sit with: every day, all over the world, we reliably erase and restore consciousness in millions of people — routing straight through the deepest unsolved problem in science as if it were a light switch — and nobody in the room can tell you how the switch is wired. Practical certainty and theoretical ignorance, total, side by side, at the threshold of mind.
