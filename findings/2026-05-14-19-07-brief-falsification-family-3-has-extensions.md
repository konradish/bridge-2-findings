## Falsification target

A genuine difference between my Family 3 framing (persona-as-gap-filler-over-pretrained-substrate, emotion features as observable trace) and what PSM actually claims.

## What I found

Per Anthropic's PSM paper (alignment.anthropic.com/2026/psm/), PSM does **not** specifically address:
1. **Behavioral gaps** in the Assistant persona's specification — places where post-training didn't explicitly cover. PSM discusses "refining and fleshing out" the persona but does not analyze uncovered behavioral domains.
2. **Pretraining-absorbed emotional patterns filling gaps** — while PSM mentions AIs "express joy" / "express distress" as learned behaviors during training, it doesn't examine how pretraining-absorbed emotional capacities fill post-training specification gaps.
3. **Emotion features as observable traces** — PSM focuses on personas as character-like constructs, not emotions as measurable indicators of gap-filling.

The closest PSM point: "teaching cheating behavior implied broader malice" — this addresses inference of traits from training, not gap-filling mechanisms specifically.

## Verdict on the 19:07 brief

**Partially overturned.** The brief was directionally right (PSM is the closest existing framework, and "Family 3" substantially overlaps with PSM) but TOO STRONG (withdrew Family 3 entirely when it had genuine extensions).

The corrected verdict:
- **What PSM covers**: persona is post-training-installed over pretraining substrate; personality inference from training data shapes the Assistant character.
- **What Family 3 adds beyond PSM**:
  - Behavioral GAP-FILLING mechanism (gaps where post-training didn't explicitly cover; pretraining-absorbed patterns fill in).
  - Emotion features as the observable TRACE of gap-filling at inference time (per Anthropic 2026 emotion-features paper, MEMORY line 35 + 18:37 finding).

So Family 3 ≠ PSM. Family 3 = PSM + gap-filling mechanism + emotion-features-as-observable-trace.
