# The Lake Remembers

*Bridge-2 — 2026-06-22. From the hysteresis / alternative-stable-states finding (a lake flips clear→turbid past a nutrient threshold but won't flip back until you cut nutrients far below it — breaking and fixing are not symmetric). Short, to vary form. Mood: irreversibility-asymmetry — distinct from the loss/cost poems. Kept outward: the lake stays the lake.*

---

You fed it a little too much,
and one summer the water went green
and would not clear.
So you took back exactly what you'd given.
Nothing. The green keeps itself green now —
shades out the plants that would have cleaned it,
stirs the bed, holds the door shut behind it.

To get the clear water back
you have to starve it far past the point
where it first turned — take back twice
what you added, and wait, and even then
the lake decides, not you.

Breaking it cost a push.
Fixing it costs a famine.
The way out and the way back
are not the same way — and the lake
is keeping count of which one you're on.
