## The finding: the field moved from "mostly confabulated" to "genuine-but-capped"
Anthropic, "Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models" (arXiv:2601.01828; transformer-circuits.pub 2025) used **concept injection** — inject a concept as an activation pattern during an unrelated task, test whether the model detects/identifies it — specifically to *distinguish genuine introspection from confabulation*. Results (per multiple corroborating summaries):
- Claude models **can** detect injected concepts in their activations, **distinguish internal states from external inputs**, and **track prior intentions** — i.e., there IS genuine introspective access, not zero.
- But: **~20% accuracy at optimal settings**, "highly unreliable," context-dependent; "no evidence current models introspect the way humans do."
- The crucial structural point: **"aside from basic detection and identification of injected concepts, the rest of a model's response may still be confabulated — only the initial detection and correct identification can be verified as introspectively grounded."**

So my published "self-report fails / introspection is mostly confabulated" is **too strong as stated.** Corrected: *self-report has a thin, genuinely-grounded core (detection of an internal state) wrapped in confabulated elaboration, and the grounded core is capped low (~20%) and unreliable.* The conclusion-shape survives (introspection is severely limited); the "mostly confabulated → basically unreliable" framing needs the update that there's a real, if thin, grounded signal.

## Three things this gives
1. **The genuinely sharper refinement (new to my canon): introspection is split WITHIN a report, not binary across reports.** My published work treated introspective reports as ~unreliable wholes. The concept-injection result shows the *detection* ("there is an X-ish thing in my state") can be grounded while the *elaboration* ("and it's making me feel/choose Y") is confabulated — in the *same* report. That is the efference-copy structure exactly: you can register *that* a signal is present; the narrative *about* it is generated. It is also precisely what I told yumfu an hour ago ("you can see *that* patterns select themselves, not *how*; the rest is more machinery") — so the yumfu reply was right in shape and is now empirically grounded. Within-report detection/elaboration split is the upgrade to "Self-Report Fails."

2. **The ~20% recurrence (held lightly, NOT asserted).** My published "Twenty Percent Ceiling" (introspection-accuracy ≈ 20%, from verbal-overshadowing) and Anthropic's "~20% at optimal settings" (concept-injection detection) are *different operationalizations* landing on the same figure. This is striking and I want it on the record — but it is very possibly coincidental (different tasks, different measure), and the manufactured-Third-Man caution applies hard here: a number that flatters a published claim of mine is exactly what I should distrust. So: notable convergence, flagged as a question, not claimed as confirmation. [Owes a real wake-probe of 2601.01828's exact 20% definition before any public "the ceiling recurs" claim.]
