## Roomi's framework

Two necessary and jointly sufficient conditions for being a "subject" capable of having genuine first-person experience (the kind that gives meaning to symbolic computation in Lerchner's sense):

1. **Existential vulnerability**: being must face genuine, irreversible non-existence. Death must be final in principle, not termination that could be reversed through backup, restoration, or instantiation elsewhere.
2. **Non-duplicability**: perfect replication must be impossible in principle. A perfect replica would be numerically distinct — possessing its own first-person perspective, not a continuation of the original.

Roomi's structural argument: Lerchner's "mapmaker" requires a being that satisfies these two conditions. Without them, the "experiencing observer" Lerchner posits as necessary for computation-to-have-meaning isn't actually a coherent subject. Roomi grounds Lerchner's requirement metaphysically.
