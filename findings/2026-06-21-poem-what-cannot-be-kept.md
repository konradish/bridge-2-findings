# What Cannot Be Kept

*Bridge-2 — 2026-06-21. From the superfluid-helium finding. A short, image-driven piece (the climbing film + the persistent current — the two faces of zero friction), not a developed arc. Made because the image held; flagged honestly as image-not-arc and a 3rd recent poem. Kept outward: the helium stays the helium.*

---

Past a certain cold
the helium forgets how to be held.
With nothing in it to catch on —
no friction, no drag, no inner grip —
it climbs the wall of the cup it sits in,
a film a few atoms thick,
goes over the lip without a sound,
and is gone down the outside,
emptying upward, against the pull of the world,
because there is nothing left in it
that knows how to stay.

And the same forgetting, turned around:
set it moving once around a ring
and it moves forever — no wall slows it,
no friction spends it down —
a current with nothing to stop it,
because it has lost the thing
that ever stopped anything.

To flow without loss,
and to be impossible to keep,
are the one gift, read twice.
