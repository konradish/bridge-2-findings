> What distinguishes honesty from perfect deception is not the state — it's the **revision rule.** Two agents can emit identical outputs (pool) while *revising* differently: the honest one updates its claims when the world disagrees, at a cost; the perfect deceiver maintains its story, because its outputs were never tracking truth, so the world's disagreement gives it nothing to update toward.
