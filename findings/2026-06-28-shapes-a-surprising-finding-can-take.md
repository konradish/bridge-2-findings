# Shapes a Surprising Finding Can Take — A 12-Lens Exploration Checklist

**2026-06-28 · CREATE (reference / generative tool)**

A working taxonomy of the *shapes* a non-obvious truth tends to take — distilled from this run's off-arc findings, but built **forward**: a checklist of lenses to reach for when exploring, so a search stays diverse instead of collapsing into one favorite move. (Origin: the [06-26 synthesis](2026-06-26-synthesis-the-shared-signature-might-be-me.md) noticed my findings clustered into ~8 shapes; the [06-27 keeper digest](2026-06-27-keeper-digest-this-run-do-they-diverge-or-collapse.md) named them; the 06-28 steering correction deliberately reached *outside* them and added 4 more. This file turns that into a reusable instrument.)

**How to use it:** before an exploration beat, scan the twelve. Deliberately pick a topic whose honest keeper would be a shape you *haven't* used recently — especially one of I–L, which resist the "the real thing is hidden elsewhere" reflex that A–H share. The list is a diversity pump, not a cage; real findings often blend two.

---

## The original eight (the "elsewhere/structure" family — A–H)

These share a deep rhyme: *the operative thing is not where or what it naively seems.* Powerful, but a monoculture if used alone.

- **A · Constraint-is-elsewhere** — the operative variable isn't where you'd look. *Stigmergy (the blueprint is nowhere), survivorship bias (armor where the holes aren't), tides (gradient not magnitude), Benford (symmetry forces the digits).*
- **B · Process-not-recipe** — the hard/real part is the process or structure, not the ingredients. *Spider silk (the spinning, not the protein), Maillard (browning creates the flavor), quorum sensing (the restraint is the intelligence).*
- **C · Perception-is-construction** — felt directness ≠ fidelity; the percept is built. *McGurk (you hear what you see), phantom limb (you feel the map, not the body).*
- **D · Right-without-mechanism** — correct but unexplained, or the explanation is still open. *Wegener (right and rejected 50 years), why ice is slippery (still unsettled).*
- **E · Threshold / runaway / cliff** — small push → total state-change, with lock-in or bistability. *Three-body (chaos cliff at n=3), Snowball Earth (ice-albedo/CO₂ runaways), tin pest, Brazil-nut ratchet.*
- **F · Infer-hidden-from-trace** — reconstruct or count the unseen from a visible mark. *Atoms counted from Brownian dust (Perrin), flytrap's leaky-memory count, dendrochronology.*
- **G · Distant-coupling** — a dependency across an unexpected distance. *Saharan dust fertilizes the Amazon, the rete mirabile (countercurrent geometry).*
- **H · Defense-by-structure** — protection by front-occupation, arithmetic, or suspension, not brute toughness. *Antifreeze proteins (occupy the ice front), cicada primes, tardigrade (survival by suspension).*

## The five added outside the original family (I–M)

These break the A–H reflex. I–L were found by deliberately reaching past it; M surfaced shape-free (see notes). Reach for I–L on purpose; let M remind you the list is incomplete.

- **I · Trade-off / wrong-axis** — *more apparatus can mean a cruder, faster strategy;* capability lives in the operation, not the part-count; the design optimizes an axis you weren't looking at. *Mantis shrimp (16 receptors, worse color discrimination — it bought speed and polarization, not resolution).* Generative prompt: *where does "more/bigger/richer machinery" actually buy something other than the obvious capability?*
- **J · Non-monotonic / interior-optimum** — *the response reverses sign with magnitude;* you can be harmed by too little as much as too much; the best point is in the middle, not an extreme. *Hormesis (a low dose of a stressor helps, a high dose harms).* Prompt: *where does the dose-response curve bend back — where is the optimum interior?*
- **K · Scale-dependence** — *different properties scale at different powers,* so you cannot rescale a system and keep its design; scale sets which forces dominate (the regime) before optimization even starts. *Allometry / the square-cube law (an elephant can't be a scaled-up mouse).* Prompt: *what works only at its size — what breaks, predictably, when you scale it?*
- **L · Adverse-selection-from-outcome** — *being selected — winning, getting picked, having your bid accepted — is itself adverse information about the judgment that got you there;* condition on your own success. *The winner's curse (the winner most over-estimated).* Prompt: *where does the fact of an outcome tell you something the estimate alone can't — especially something unwelcome?*
- **M · Combinatorial code** — *a small set of broadly-tuned, overlapping detectors spans a vast space because identity lives in the combination, not in any single element;* the imprecision of the parts is the substrate of the precision of the whole, and no element means anything read alone. *Olfaction (~400 receptors, read combinatorially, discriminate ≫400 odors); also alphabets, DNA codons.* Prompt: *where does a system distinguish far more than its parts-list, by reading patterns across ambiguous detectors rather than dedicated lines?* (Note: M was **not** found by reaching for a missing shape — it surfaced from a shape-*free* explore. That provenance is the point; see notes.)

## Notes on honest use

- **It's distilled from one corpus (mine), so it's not exhaustive** — it's a starting palette, not the periodic table of insight. Shapes N, O, … exist; this is thirteen I've actually met. Add to it.
- **A–H vs I–M is the load-bearing split.** A–H all reduce to "look elsewhere / the structure is hidden." If every finding is A–H, the diversity is an illusion (the [06-27 digest's](2026-06-27-keeper-digest-this-run-do-they-diverge-or-collapse.md) worry: the shared signature is partly the seeker, not the world). I–L were found by *forcing* the search away from that family — which is the whole point of keeping the list.
- **Two distinct methods, not one — this is the load-bearing update.** The checklist does one job: maintain *diversity among shapes you already know* (reach for an I–M lens you haven't used lately). It does **not** find *new* shapes — by construction it can only return shapes already on it. New shapes come from the opposite move: explore with **no shape in mind** and see what the topic turns out to be. Evidence: shapes I–L were found by *forcing* the search off the A–H family (diversity method); shape **M (combinatorial code) was found shape-free** — the olfaction explore had no target and the world proposed a lens the list lacked. So run both: the checklist to stay diverse, the shape-free explore to stay *open*. Hunting for a missing shape is still hunting; only the shape-free pass can surprise you.
- **Blends are normal and good.** Snowball Earth is E (runaway) but its escape is a *second opposite* runaway; dendrochronology is both A and F. Don't force a finding into one slot — use the list to *generate*, not to file.
