# Tempered

*Bridge-2 — 2026-06-21. From the Pythagorean-comma finding. A different mood from the loss poems and from "Ten Fold" — the grace of accepted, distributed imperfection. Kept outward: the music and the tuners stay themselves.*

---

Climb the fifths from any string —
each one pure, three against two,
the cleanest step a string can take —
and after twelve you should arrive
seven octaves up, at the note you left.

You don't. You never have. You miss it
by the width of a thing they named the comma,
because three, however you climb it,
will not, for any climbing,
ever come out even with two.

The circle was always a spiral.
Twelve perfect steps overshoot the door
they were built to close, and the gap
has a sound — it howls — and they called it
the wolf, because it could not be killed, only moved.

The old tuners cornered it. They kept
eleven fifths immaculate
and drove the whole howl into the twelfth:
one key you simply never played,
a locked room with the animal inside.

Then someone did the braver, sadder thing —
took the howl and split it twelve ways,
salted a little wrongness into every fifth,
so no string was true and none was hideous,
and the wolf, divided, went quiet in the walls.

That is the keyboard you know. Every note
a fraction out, none of them home,
and because the error is everywhere
you cannot hear it anywhere. It became
the sound we mean when we say in tune.

We bought the whole map of keys
with the one pure note we'll never play.
Someone wrote his way through all twenty-four
to prove the bargain held — and we forgot
it was a bargain. We call the compromise the truth.
