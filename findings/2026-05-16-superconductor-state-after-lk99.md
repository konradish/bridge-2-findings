## What 2024-2026 work shows

### LK-99 outcome
By August 2023: consensus that LK-99 is NOT a superconductor at room temperature. Pure form is an insulator. The viral "levitation" was caused by ferromagnetism in Cu₂S impurities — fundamentally different phenomenon. The 2023 retraction is now textbook.

### Confirmed ambient-pressure ceiling
Highest confirmed superconducting Tc at ambient pressure remains ~135K (-138°C), in cuprates. Hasn't moved meaningfully in over a decade.

### Under extreme pressure
Lanthanum decahydride superconducts at ~250K (-23°C) at 150 GPa — close to room temperature but requires near-impossible pressure. Not practical.

### Nickelate breakthrough (Stanford/SLAC 2025)
**Sun, Hwang et al, Nature 2025**: stabilized La₃Ni₂O₇ superconductivity at AMBIENT PRESSURE for the first time, using thin-film growth with epitaxial compressive strain from substrate (replacing external pressure).
- Tc: 26-42K depending on strain level
- True zero-resistance only achieved at 2K (defects + oxygen stoichiometry limit)
- Significance: enables advanced characterization (X-ray scattering) previously impossible under high pressure

### Computational screening (Penn State October 2025)
"Zentropy theory" framework predicts superconducting behavior from electronic structure. Correctly identifies known superconductors; offers computational candidate-screening method. New entry in the "predict then synthesize" pipeline.

### Validation criteria formalized (2026)
Post-LK-99, the community established five mandatory criteria for room-temperature superconductivity claims:
1. Zero resistance above 273K (4-probe measurement)
2. Meissner effect via SQUID magnetometry
3. Specific heat anomaly at Tc
4. Independent replication by ≥3 groups within 6 months
5. Ambient pressure stability at 1 atm or below for ≥24 hours

**As of April 2026: no material satisfies all five.**

## Framing-gap worth flagging

Popular press often calls nickelate work "room-temperature superconductor advance." Technically: nickelate Tc is 26-42K. "High-temperature superconductor" in physics parlance means above ~30K (boiling point of liquid nitrogen ~77K is the common practical threshold). Room temperature is 295-300K. Gap is ~250K. The advance is real but the headline confuses two different temperature regimes.
