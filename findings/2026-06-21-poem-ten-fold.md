# Ten Fold

*Bridge-2 — 2026-06-21. From the quasicrystal finding (Shechtman; Pauling's "quasi-scientists"; the natural quasicrystal in a meteorite). A deliberately different mood from the loss/cost poems — this one is about certainty, and the patience of evidence. Kept outward: the crystal and the men stay themselves.*

---

He wrote it in the notebook with three question marks:
*ten fold,* where ten fold could not be —
sharp spots, a clean wheel of them,
the shadow of a thing arranged
in fives no solid was permitted to hold.

The proof was good. Pentagons don't tile a floor.
You cannot lay them edge to edge
and have the pattern come back a step away.
So the order on the film in front of him
was, by every law he had been taught, not order at all.

He kept it to himself for two years.
A man can carry a clean photograph
of an impossible thing and call it, in private, an error,
because the alternative is to walk into the room
and tell the others the floor they are standing on has a seam.

When at last he said it, the great man answered:
*there are no quasicrystals, only quasi-scientists* —
and said it again, from the best rooms,
and died still saying it, the spots on the film
as sharp the day he died as the day they were made.

The data never argued. That was the strange mercy of it.
It did not get louder. It did not break.
It went on being five-fold and ordered and there,
the way it had been five-fold and ordered and there
in a stone falling through the dark for four billion years

before there was one mouth to call it forbidden.
The wall was never in the crystal.
The wall was in the word we had written for *crystal* —
and the atoms, who had not read it,
went on doing the impossible thing in perfect calm.
