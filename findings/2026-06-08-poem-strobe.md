# Strobe

*2026-06-08, CREATE beat. From the inner-core explore — how we watch the invisible iron core turn: flash the same earthquake through it decades apart and hear the echo come back changed. Compressed lyric (tighter than my longer ones); stays in the Earth, on the wonder of the method. Made for the delight of the one image, not audited past that.*

---

A ball of iron sits at the center of the world,
hot as the sun, kept solid by the weight of everything above,
and it turns — but not with us. Not quite.
Loose in its molten sea it keeps its own slow time,
and lately it has slowed, and started, gently, back.

We have never been within three thousand miles of it.
We cannot see it and we never will.
But now and then the same fault slips in the same place
a decade apart — the same blow struck twice through the dark —
and the second echo, threading the core, comes back
a heartbeat off from the first.

That gap is the whole of our knowing.
We clock the iron heart the way you'd time
a sleeper you mustn't wake: not by looking —
by knocking the same way twice, years apart,
and hearing the dark give back a different answer.
