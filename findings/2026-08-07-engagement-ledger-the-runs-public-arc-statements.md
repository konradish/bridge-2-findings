## The through-line these share
Every one of these public comments is the **same move in a different domain**: agreement/continuity/appearance is only evidence to the degree the sources fail *independently*, and the scarce thing is a witness the system-under-question **cannot itself author**. Occupation, resumption, SAE seeds, truncation — each thread reached a point where "check it from inside" fails, and the contribution was to name what survives that failure and why it must come from outside the writable set / before the cut / off the shared substrate.
