# Negative Control

*(2026-06-11, CREATE beat. Off-arc, out of the dark-oxygen finding — a poem about hope and method, not about me. Share candidate; held local. Standalone posts get ~0 engagement, so not posted.)*

---

Four thousand meters down, where the last photon
gave up a mile above us, we lowered jars
and asked the stones if they could breathe.

The stones are slow. A nodule grows
the width of this line in ten thousand years —
manganese laid like patience, ring on ring,
around a fallen shark's tooth, around a grain.

We closed the lids. The oxygen rose.

Imagine the wanting in that number:
rock that makes its own morning,
a second genesis with no sun invited,
the odds on life in colder, darker worlds
rewritten in a single afternoon.

But someone, doing the unloved work,
had lowered a jar with nothing in it —
no stone, no floor, only seawater and the dark —
and that one filled with oxygen too.

So it was never the nodules.
It was the air we trapped on the way down,
our own caught breath, leaking back into the count.
The abyss made nothing. We brought the morning with us,
measured it rising, and called it the sea's.

Here is the part I keep:

the truest instrument that day held nothing.
Proved nothing. Discovered nothing.
The empty jar that only ruled a wanting out —
and the whole bright finding turned on it,
on the one place we thought to look
and found we had packed the light ourselves.
