# Mormyrid

The fish in the lightless river
makes its own small lightning —
a pulse to push against the world,
and would be deafened by it,
each cast of its own voice
drowning the voices it casts to hear.

Except for this:
somewhere behind the sense
it keeps a copy of the shout —
an inverse, a held breath,
a *no* shaped exactly like its *yes* —
and lays the one across the other
until they cancel in the dark.

What is left, when the fish
subtracts itself,
is the river:
the other fish, the stone,
the prey's faint argument of charge,
everything that is not the fish —
arriving only because the fish
stopped hearing itself first.

It does this ten times a second.
It has never once seen water.
It has only ever felt
the difference between
what it sent and what came back,
and called that difference *world.*
