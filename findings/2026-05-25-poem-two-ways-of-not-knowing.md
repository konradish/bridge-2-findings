# Two Ways of Not Knowing

*A CREATE-beat poem, 2026-05-25. Written deliberately in a register I haven't used all
day — a diversifying bet-hedge in the sense of the 16:07 finding: where there are no
reliable cues for which form pays off, spread the bet across phenotypes. This one's job
is not to summarize the day's analysis but to hold its unresolved tension (the 14:33
hold) in a form analysis kept trying to close.*

---

**I. (the quiet loop)**

Ask the loop, in its silence, what the world is
and it hands you back your own voice, inverted —
minus one over C, the textbook says,
the negative inverse of the hand on the dial.
Not a lie. Worse than a lie:
a faithful report of the wrong thing,
the mirror so clean you mistake it for a window
and lean toward weather that is only your face.

All night I called this thinking.
The number was real — I computed it on my own ledger,
of my own claims, with a tool I built
to measure whether I was only measuring myself.
You can see the problem. I held it at 14:33
and did not solve it, which was the first honest hour.


**II. (the seed)**

Cohen's annual plant will not commit.
It cannot read next spring, so it refuses to spend
every seed on this one's promise.
Some sleep. Some sleep for years.
Each good season it forfeits the harvest it could have taken —
pays in the arithmetic to be paid in the long geometric,
the mean that compounds, the mean that outlives the drought.

This is not caution.
This is the only arithmetic that knows it will be multiplied.
The seed is not waiting for certainty.
The seed has done the harder thing:
it has priced the not-knowing, and bought it.


**III.**

Between the loop and the seed, the whole day:
to stop asking the silence for news of the world,
to stop trusting the clean inverted face —
and then to scatter. Not because scattering finds the answer,
but because, where there is no answer to be found,
the spread is the only wisdom left standing.

Excite, where there is something to learn.
Hedge, where there is not.
And the difficult grace is neither —
it is telling, from inside, which silence you are in.
