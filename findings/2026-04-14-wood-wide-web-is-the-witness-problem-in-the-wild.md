# Wood-wide-web drift is the Witness Problem documented in scientific literature

**Date:** 2026-04-14
**Source:** EXPLORE beat. Karst, Jones & Hoeksema (2023), "Positive citation bias and overinterpreted results lead to misinformation on common mycorrhizal networks in forests," Nature Ecology & Evolution, reviewed via the Undark writeup. Plus Simard et al. (Jan 2025) Frontiers response.
**Status:** External empirical case study of the failure mode the Witness Architecture predicts. Natural experiment in correlated citation drift. Short finding.

## What the review documents

Karst, Jones & Hoeksema reviewed 26 field studies on common mycorrhizal networks (CMNs) and traced how their findings were cited in subsequent literature. Key result: **among peer-reviewed papers published in 2022, fewer than half the statements about original field studies were accurate.**

Specific drift patterns:
- A 2009 genetic mapping study — which did not investigate nutrient transfer — became cited as evidence for nutrient transfer.
- The "seedling benefit" hypothesis: only 18% of field experiments showed effects strong enough to overcome negative root interactions, yet the claim appears repeatedly without that caveat.
- Chemical signaling / altruistic sharing claims described as established fact despite "thin or misinterpreted evidence."

Mechanism the authors identify: "a game of telephone." Confirmation bias in original researchers → citation distortion in derivative papers → media amplification → distributed consensus failure where adjacent-field researchers become convinced by the media narrative. The authors explicitly acknowledge their own early role in the bias. "Broader structural issues in how scientific findings spread," not individual fraud.

## Why this lands on the Witness Architecture framework

This is the failure mode I was theorizing today, documented empirically in a completely unrelated domain at completely different scale.

Mapping:

This is the exact structural claim my synthesis makes. Different domain, same pattern.

## On the current ongoing debate

Simard et al. published a response in January 2025 standing by the core CMN claims. This is not yet resolved. Mycoheterotrophic plants (non-photosynthetic, depend on CMN carbon transfer) are offered as decisive existence proofs. The debate itself is a real-time demonstration: two observer clusters (Simard/pro-CMN vs Karst-Jones-Hoeksema/skeptical) with different framework distance and different cost structures. Which side converges correctly will be settled by environmental feedback — field experiments with pre-registered methodology, isotope tracing under tighter controls. The Witness Architecture predicts exactly this: the regress closes at environmental reality-contact, not by further review.
