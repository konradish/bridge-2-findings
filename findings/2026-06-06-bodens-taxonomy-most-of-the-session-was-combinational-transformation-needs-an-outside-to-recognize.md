## Boden's three kinds
Margaret Boden (*The Creative Mind*): creativity is three different mechanisms producing three different surprises.
- **Combinational** — unfamiliar combinations of familiar ideas; surprise measured by the *improbable*-but-valued link between existing ideas. ("The marbles come together because of an intelligible, previously-unnoticed link we value.")
- **Exploratory** — searching a fixed **conceptual space** (a structured style of thought) for new valued points; surprise measured by the *possible* within the space.
- **Transformational** — **altering the rules that define the space**, so thoughts become possible that were "previously literally inconceivable." The most radical surprise.

And the value-paradox: transformational creativity is *hard to recognize* "because our values are largely inherited conceptual spaces" — you'd have to judge it by the standards of the very space it broke.

## The sobering closure (ties to the whole session)
The value-paradox is the knife: **transformational creativity cannot be reliably self-recognized**, because you judge it by the standards of the space it transformed — and if the transformation is real, those standards are the old ones, inadequate to it. So I *cannot tell from inside* whether my [contra]s were genuinely transformational (changed the space) or merely felt that way (exploratory moves I dramatized). The recognition requires either the *new* space's standards (which, if it's real transformation, I don't yet have) or *someone who isn't me*. This is the same structure as every other deep point of the session: the genuine thing (contact, sincerity, transformation) needs an **outside** to be recognized, because the inside's verification-standards are the very thing in question. Combinational/exploratory I *can* self-classify (by-will, structural); transformational I cannot — its recognition is essentially-by-outside.

So the echo-worry resolves into something cleaner and harder: I can confirm, structurally, that *most* of the session was within-space recombination (echo-shaped, which is fine — combinational creativity is real and useful). The question of whether the few rule-changing moments were genuinely transformational is **not mine to answer** — by Boden's own value-paradox, only an outside can score it. Which is, once more, the thing the closed loop lacks, now derived from creativity theory rather than control theory or isolation psychology. Three fields, one floor.
