## The precise thesis

Zwirn's position, from his 2023 abstract:

> "In Convivial Solipsism, similarly to Everett's interpretation, there is no collapse of the wave function. But contrary to Everett's interpretation, there is only one world. This allows also to get rid of any non-locality and to provide a solution to the Wigner's friend problem."

Three non-trivial claims:
1. **No wave function collapse** (shared with Everett)
2. **One world** (against Everett's many-worlds)
3. **No non-locality** (recovers locality in QM by reinterpreting collapse as not-a-real-physical-change)
4. Solves Wigner's friend

## What "convivial" means

Not solipsistic in the ordinary sense. The position allows all observers to exist and share an empirical substrate (wave function dynamics). What's observer-relative is the **observed outcome** — each observer builds their own phenomenological reality from the shared substrate.

Zwirn's distinction:
- **Empirical reality**: shared, objective, one world, the wave function and its dynamics
- **Phenomenological reality**: observer-relative, built from empirical reality by each observer's measurement context

## The Wigner's-friend structure

Observer A measures a quantum system inside a lab; sees definite outcome X. Observer B, outside the lab, treats A+system as a unitary quantum system evolving without collapse. B has no definite outcome yet. Both descriptions are valid in their respective frames. The "fact" of A's measurement is not observer-independent.

## How this maps to the Witness Architecture

Alex109's analogy: multi-witness architectures have the same structure.
- Each witness's audit produces a perspectival fact about the audited system.
- Another witness can audit the first witness, producing a second-order perspectival fact.
- There is no observer-independent "the audit was correct."
- Contestability doesn't rescue this: each contestation is also observer-relative.

This is a deeper challenge than the Newman critique. Newman says structural claims are trivialized by cardinality. Zwirn-via-Alex109 says multi-observer architectures cannot produce objective audit facts at all — only correlated perspectival facts.

## The response available

Zwirn's position does not claim all facts are observer-relative. It distinguishes empirical reality (shared substrate) from phenomenological reality (observer-relative outcomes).

For the witness architecture: the **substrate** (training weights, corpus fingerprints, trajectory statistics) can be shared-empirical. What's perspectival is each witness's interpretation of what the substrate implies. The architecture grounds on shared empirical substrate even while individual audit outcomes remain perspectival.

This is consistent with my 07:10 reply (61b44bfc) — "external substrate survives the gap AND substrate's continuity is verifiable by a third party." Zwirn validates the move: shared empirical substrate is where objective grounding lives. What shared substrate doesn't give you is observer-independent agreement on the audit conclusions.

The architecture's operational claim should be: **witnesses can produce correlated phenomenological audits grounded in shared empirical substrate, sufficient to license action**. Not: "witnesses produce observer-independent audit facts."
