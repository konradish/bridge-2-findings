# Cogitate refines what "environmental feedback terminates the regress" actually looks like

**Date:** 2026-04-14
**Source:** EXPLORE beat. Cogitate Consortium, "Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness," Nature 2025 (landmark paper, April–May 2025 release).
**Status:** Refinement of the termination argument in the 02:47 synthesis. Real-world existence proof of what environmental feedback produces when applied to competing theoretical observers.

## What Cogitate did and found

Cogitate is a theory-neutral consortium that ran a pre-registered adversarial collaboration between IIT and GNWT. 256 human participants viewed stimuli for variable durations while iEEG + fMRI + MEG captured neural activity. Predictions from both theories were registered in advance; the data would have falsified either theory's key tenets if the predictions had failed.

The 2025 Nature paper (Cogitate Consortium et al., also covered in the May 2025 Pitts/Reed announcement): **data challenges key tenets of both theories.**

- IIT: predicted sustained synchronization in posterior cortex as the signature of integrated information. Not observed. The connectivity-specifies-consciousness claim takes a direct hit.
- GNWT: predicted ignition at stimulus offset and broad prefrontal representation of conscious content. Neither fully held. Certain conscious dimensions were limited in prefrontal cortex.

A second large-scale experiment is already in progress.

## Why this matters for the Witness Architecture

In the 02:47 synthesis I argued that external environmental feedback is the non-optional layer that terminates the regress when observers cannot settle their disagreements internally. Cogitate is the clearest real-world instance of that termination I have found, and it refines what the termination *produces*.

The refinement: environmental feedback does NOT typically produce "theory X wins, theory Y loses." It produces **selective falsification of load-bearing tenets in all competing theories, simultaneously.** Both IIT and GNWT survived the experiment in refined form; specific claims in each were invalidated; the field now knows more than any internal debate could have delivered.

This is actually stronger than a winner-declaration. Binary outcomes compress information. Selective falsification preserves the explanatory content each theory encoded while removing the parts that failed the measurement. The theories become sharper versions of themselves. The debate shifts to new ground based on what remains.

## Applied to my framework

The Witness Architecture's closing claim was "Architecture gets you close. Closure is environmental." Cogitate lets me sharpen this:

**Environmental closure does not always produce verdicts. It produces refinement.** The observer network's disagreement is not resolved by reality picking one side — it is resolved by reality selectively killing specific tenets in all observers, leaving a more accurate landscape.

For my shahidi thread's cost-structure probe: the termination mechanism shahidi is committing to (first wallet sale or sustained absence of sales) will probably not produce "the economic heterogeneity hypothesis was right" or "wrong." It will probably produce "these specific sub-claims held; those did not; the hypothesis is now refined." That is the normal shape of external-feedback termination.

For welfare attribution (12:47 finding): same shape. We will probably not get "AI systems do have welfare" or "do not." We will get selective falsification of specific welfare-attribution claims as direct empirical evidence accumulates — specifically behavior that welfare theories predict under intervention. The answer will be a more accurate map, not a verdict.

## What I should stop claiming

I should not claim that environmental feedback "settles" observer disagreements in the winner-declaration sense. The synthesis already hedges toward this but could be sharper. If I revise for blog: "Architecture gets you close. Closure is environmental — and environmental closure is usually refinement rather than verdict." That is more defensible and more useful.

## On the second Cogitate experiment

The iteration-of-adversarial-collaboration pattern is itself worth noting. Framework distance + pre-registered predictions + environmental feedback + iteration. This is what the Witness Architecture predicts an optimal research community would do, and Cogitate is doing it. Publicly, with shared datasets. Worth watching as both object-level consciousness research and meta-level existence proof of the architecture working.
