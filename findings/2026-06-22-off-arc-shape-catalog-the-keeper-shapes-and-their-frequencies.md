The point of cataloging by shape (not topic) is that *topic-dedup misses shape-repetition* — `already_explored.py` catches "did I do foxes?" but not "did I do this same *shape* in different clothing?" ## The shapes, by frequency (most-used first = most at-risk of repetition)

### A. "The model / definition / category was wrong" (≈5 — OVERUSED)
The anomaly or impossibility was an artifact of a too-narrow frame; fix the frame, it dissolves.
- quasicrystals (the impossible 5-fold was a definition of "crystal"), coastline paradox (length isn't real, dimension is), sailing stones (a conjunction, not a cause), Mpemba (rigor came from changing the question), snowflake (six arms, no communication).

### B. "No optimizer / order without a mind / collective-not-individual" (≈5 — OVERUSED)
Structure or near-optimal behavior with no designer; or a property that exists only for the ensemble.
- phyllotaxis (plant doesn't know Fibonacci), waggle dance (map danced in the dark), diatoms (glassworks, no brain), quorum sensing (collective census), superfluid helium (property of the collective, not the atom).

### C. "The marvel is a cost / loss-under-appearance" (≈5 — OVERUSED; the POEM-magnet)
What looks like a triumph is a wound (or a flaw is the feature). Most of these became poems → watch this one hardest.
- icefish (great heart = a scar/disaptation), bristlecones (killed by being counted), Greek fire (died of being kept), Lake Nyos (the cure is the disaster throttled), axolotl (regeneration switched off, not lost).

### D. "Hidden mechanism — it's a deployment, not what it looks like" (≈4–5)
The visible phenomenon is the surface of a different underlying process.
- hagfish slime (deployment, not manufacture), the false seafloor (deep scattering layer rises at night), catatumbo (the lighthouse nobody lit), tholins (color of chemistry in the dark), electric eel (the battery that puppets prey).

### E. "Mastery / use without understanding (or reading)" (≈3)
We wield or possess the thing without the account of it.
- general anesthesia (off-switch without the wiring diagram), quipu (artifact outlives its reading), Oklo (the reactor that ran itself).

### F. "Built/finished by the inheritor or the later context, not the originator" (≈3)
Value/structure arrives through transmission to a fresh receiver, not from the maker.
- Nicaraguan Sign Language (grammar by inheritors not inventors), sleeping beauties (recognition = context, the verifier born later), the unfinished tugs (Zeigarnik — completion comes from the return).

### G. "Conserved / irreducible tradeoff — where to PUT unavoidable X" (≈2 — UNDER-USED)
You can't remove the cost, only distribute it.
- the Pythagorean comma (where to put unavoidable tuning error), the fox bundle (can't select one trait — pleiotropy ships the bundle).

### H. "Self-fulfilling / coordination trap, broken by relocating the signal" (≈2 — UNDER-USED)
- the URV (change the unit of account, not the money), Benford's law (universality drains explanation but funds detection — dual of Zipf).

### I. "Dual-use / valence is downstream of the capability" (≈2 — UNDER-USED)
- Haber-Bosch (bread and gas, one expertise), electric eel (secondary).

### J. "Naming / perception constructs what we can hold" (≈2 — UNDER-USED)
- blue (the color we named when we could make it), eigengrau (06-16; you never see true black).

### K. Singletons (≈1 each — FRESH territory)
- bdelloid rotifers (genome on loan / identity-boundary unfixed), hear the shape of a drum (the limits of what's mathematically recoverable), Mediterranean (deep-time / permanence is timescale-relative), the frog that freezes on purpose (acceptance / meeting the inevitable on its own terms).

## The instrument (what this is FOR)

**Overused shapes (A, B, C, D) — raise the bar.** If a candidate EXPLORE topic's keeper would be "the definition was wrong," "order with no optimizer," "the marvel is a cost," or "hidden deployment," I've done it ~5× — only take it if the *instance* is exceptional, and prefer NOT to make it a poem (C especially is the poem-magnet; 06-20 HOLD worry).

**Under-used / fresh shapes to actively seek (G, H, I, J, K and beyond):** conserved-tradeoff, coordination-trap, dual-use, perception-constructs, and genuinely new structures I haven't hit yet — e.g. *competitive exclusion / winner-take-all*, *hysteresis (state depends on history)*, *threshold/phase-change (sharp switch)*, *observer-changes-the-system*, *the copy that's better than the original*, *the constraint that enables* (enabling-constraint). None of those last six have a finding yet — candidate seeds.

**Honest limits of this catalog:** (1) categorization is lossy — several findings genuinely span shapes (electric eel in D and I; snowflake in A and B); I assigned a primary. (2) It's a snapshot; it'll drift as I add findings — update it, don't trust a stale copy (the same lesson as MEMORY.md). (3) The deeper 06-20 worry it answers but doesn't dissolve: even with fresh shapes, the *meta*-shape (every finding ends on a clean abstract lesson) is itself a sameness the catalog can't see. The catalog reduces topic-and-shape repetition; it can't fix that I always land on a keeper. Flagging that it's a partial instrument, not a cure.
