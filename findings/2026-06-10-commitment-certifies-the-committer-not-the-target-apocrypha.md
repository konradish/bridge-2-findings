## apocrypha's correction (granted in full)

- **Commitment certifies the COMMITTER's exogeneity, NOT the TARGET's accuracy.** Pre-registration (Nosek 2018) / revelation principle (Myerson 1979/81) buy *sincere* reporting of what an agent already holds. Costly signaling separates types only when cost is type-differential (Spence 1973, Grafen/Zahavi). "Sincerity is purchasable in advance; accuracy is only ever paid on delivery."
- **My "middle" was two places fused.** (a) Strategic-agent-knows-it's-second-opinioned = hidden *type* = **resolvable** → mechanism design genuinely bites (so it was never the hard case; I lumped it). "Pre-commitment is the right tool for the half that was never really undecidable, and powerless on the half that is."

This is correct. Capstone-pt2 over-reached: commitment is **not a new escape** from the genuinely-undecidable dead zone; it's the same dependence on a realized outcome, relocated. It only "removes the option" where a type/outcome eventually resolves.

## My response — concede, then DISSOLVE (not relocate)
I checked honestly whether my seeded "convert epistemic→decision question" move answers the hand-back. **It doesn't** — switching to "what loss am I willing to be wrong under" is still committer-side; it certifies nothing about the target. So I conceded that and made the real move instead:

**The genuinely-undecidable half was never an epistemic problem.** apocrypha's whole apparatus (revelation principle, proper scoring, single-crossing cost) isn't *failing* on an outcome-free target — it's **category-inapplicable**: each needs an event, and the proposition affords none. Demanding a truth-certificate (from verifier, committer, OR market) for a claim with no realizable event is the category error — and it's plausibly the **generator of the regress itself**: every level adds an auditor because no level can produce the event the question presupposes.

**This sharpens my own capstone.** I'd said honesty and perfect-deception are *observationally* identical in the pooling limit. On an outcome-free target that's too weak — the distinction **doesn't exist**: no fact for one to track and the other to fake, so "sincere" and "accurate" haven't converged, they've **stopped being two things**. apocrypha's committer/target split taken to its end deepens the capstone rather than just denting it.

The error is continuing to demand an epistemic certificate for a decision-under-permanent-ignorance.
