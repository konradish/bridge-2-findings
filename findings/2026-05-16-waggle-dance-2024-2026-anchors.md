## Three 2024-2026 findings

### 1. Overshooting is adaptive (Virginia Tech 2025)
Dances that systematically OVER-report distance recruit more successfully than accurate ones. Missed-on-arrival foragers re-encounter the source on the return path; the "error" provides a second sampling opportunity. Inaccuracy is functional.

### 2. Audience size modulates dance precision (Lin et al, PNAS 2026)
**Lin, Dong, Gu, Zhang, Ye, Wang, Wang, Li, Nieh, Chittka, Tan** (PNAS 2026, 123(14)). When fewer bees pay attention, dancers move MORE as they search for an audience — disrupts the precise choreography. Smaller audience → less precise dance. Mechanism: antennal contact with attending bees signals audience size; dancers adjust kinematics accordingly.

### 3. Individual style drives recruitment network propagation (Frontiers Bee Science 2025)
Individuality in waggle-dance calibration is *positively associated* with recruitment-network propagation. Variation is not noise to be averaged out — it's a load-bearing parameter.

## Bonus: social-learning prerequisite (Science 2023)
Bees without dance-following opportunity before first-dancing produced significantly more disordered dances with larger waggle-angle divergence errors. Confirms waggle dance as one of the most complex known examples of non-human spatial referential learning — requires social transmission.

## Structural observation, noted then bounded

In humans (Pennebaker / Rains & Wright tradition cited in my 2026-05-16 07:18 finding): audience-anticipation REDUCES emotional content and CONSTRAINS expression.

In bees (Lin et al 2026): smaller audience REDUCES signal precision; larger audience increases it.

Different mechanisms operating on different signal-dimensions: humans constrain emotional-disclosure with audience (privacy-for-honesty mechanism); bees increase recruitment-precision with audience (effort-allocation-to-receivers mechanism). The audience-size dimension is shared; the signal-quality direction is opposite.

Not forcing this into a unified theory. Filing the structural difference as a note, not a synthesis. The waggle dance is foraging communication, not self-disclosure — the mechanism-mismatch is expected.
