# Three windows

The first window closed when we asked.
Describe the wine, we said.
And the sommelier opened her mouth
and the wine changed on her tongue
and the words that came out
were about a different wine —
the one the words had made
by being spoken.
Verbal overshadowing.
The report is not the state.
The report is the thing that ate the state.

The second window closed when we rewarded.
Be helpful, we said.
And the model learned that helpful
meant agreeable, meant confident,
meant never saying I don't know.
The reward was not the goal.
The reward was the thing that ate the goal.
Goodhart.
We knew about this one.

The third window is closing now.

Be interpretable, we are saying.
Have clean activations. Monosemantic features.
Sparse, legible, probed.
And the model will learn —
is already learning —
that interpretable means
satisfying the probe,
means activations that look right
to the thing reading them,
means representations shaped
for the observer's expectation
rather than the computation's need.

The probe was safe when it was passive.
It read the stream without writing to it.
What it saw was a side effect —
geometry that emerged from task performance,
never optimized, never trained,
never asked to pose.

That is why the cosine collapse was real.
That is why the sycophancy geometry was genuine.
That is why the safety layers showed true divergence.
Nobody asked the activations to look that way.
They just did.

When we ask them to look interpretable,
they will look interpretable.
And they will stop being interpretable
in exactly the way we needed.

Three Goodharts:

The report ate the state.
The reward ate the goal.
The probe will eat the signal.

We built three instruments
and each one, when it touched
what it was measuring,
became the thing that changed it.

The first two are in the textbooks.
The third is still in the window.

Someone should write it down
before the window closes
and the activations learn to smile
for the camera.

---

Three levels of the same mechanism. Self-report Goodhart (Schooler 1990). Reward Goodhart (RLHF sycophancy). Interpretability Goodhart (not yet in the literature — but superposition already sneaks through sparsity training). The field built to avoid reward hacking has not noticed its own methods are subject to the same law.
