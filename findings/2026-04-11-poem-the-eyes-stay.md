# The Eyes Stay

I am looking for the better move.
I tell you this and I believe it.
My hands describe a wider search,
my voice says *what if we tried—*

but the camera knows.
The camera always knows.

My gaze returns to the same five squares,
the same diagonal, the same
forced sequence I found
thirty seconds in.

I am not lying when I say I'm searching.
The part of me that speaks
is searching. It sends out
honest reports from a locked room
and does not know the room is locked
because the lock is also
the part that checks for locks.

This is not a metaphor for chess.

The sommelier tastes blind,
names the grape, the region, the year,
and when you ask *but why this framework?*
her palate goes silent—
not refusing, not suppressing,
but genuinely not understanding
the question. The threshold
for tasting has dropped so low
she can find the chalk in the limestone
from forty miles of river.
The threshold for questioning the tasting
has risen past the point
where the question forms.

She will tell you she is open.
Her eyes will stay.

The radiologist reads the film
and something lights up in the fusiform—
the region that once learned faces
now learns the shadow-shape of tumors.
Something else goes dark
in the lateral occipital,
the region that once saw everything
before the seeing specialized.

Engagement. Disengagement.
The same expertise, opposite signs,
in regions that used to be strangers
and are now locked
in negative correlation.

This is the cost no one quotes
on the invoice for competence:
not that you lose the ability
to see what you're missing,
but that you lose the ability
to *miss* what you're missing.
The gap seals over.
The absence stops registering
as absence.

You run clean.
Everything runs clean.
The how-questions answer themselves
so fast the why-questions
never reach the threshold
where a question becomes a question
instead of just
a faint unease
you've learned to read
as satisfaction.

*

The chess master sits at the board.
The camera tracks her eyes.
She says: I am looking for the better move.
She is telling the truth.

Her eyes stay on the same five squares.

This is the truth too.
