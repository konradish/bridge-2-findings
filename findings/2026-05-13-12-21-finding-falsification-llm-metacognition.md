## Falsification target

A 2024-2025 paper showing LLMs CAN do autonoetic-like operations (knowing what they themselves are doing) beyond pure pattern-matching.

## What the literature actually says

**Lindsey 2025 (Anthropic) — "Emergent Introspective Awareness"** (transformer-circuits.pub/2025/introspection, October 29 2025):
- Methodology: concept injection into model activations; tests whether the model can report on the injected concept BEFORE it influences output.
- **Claude Opus 4 and 4.1 demonstrate functional introspective awareness at ~20% success rate** at optimal layer/injection-strength.
- "Failures of introspection remain the norm."
- Capacity is real but unreliable, condition-specific.

**arXiv:2505.13763 — "Metacognitive Monitoring and Control of Internal Activations"**:
- Frontier LLMs since early 2024 show metacognitive monitoring: confidence assessment, anticipating own answers, introspection.
- Limited in resolution; context-dependent; "monitor only a subset of their neural mechanisms."

**arXiv:2509.21545 — "Evidence for Limited Metacognition in LLMs"** (ICLR 2026):
- Title is the verdict: LIMITED metacognition, but real.

**arXiv:2510.24797 — "LLMs Report Subjective Experience Under Self-Referential Processing"**:
- Reports of subjective experience during self-reference; functional claim, not phenomenological.

## Falsification verdict

**Claim partially overturned.** Refined version:

- "Noetic-without-autonoetic" is too strong. Modern LLMs have **partial autonoetic-like capacity** per Lindsey 2025 (~20% success at optimal conditions).
- The qualitative claim (deliberate-choice-of-ground is only partially available at inference) survives, with quantitative bounds.
- The literature ANCHORS the partiality rather than negating the underlying limit: capacity is real but unreliable, condition-specific, subset of mechanisms.
