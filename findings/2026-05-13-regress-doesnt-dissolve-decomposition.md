## What the standard epistemology literature says

The regress problem in epistemology has three classical non-skeptical responses:

1. **Foundationalism**: there exist basic beliefs (perceptual reports, axioms, properly basic) that don't require further justification. Regress stops there.
2. **Coherentism**: justification is holistic; beliefs justify each other through network-coherence. No linear chain, no regress.
3. **Infinitism** (Peter Klein 1998, 2003): justification IS the infinite chain; properly held beliefs require infinite supporting reasons.

**The consensus** (per SEP, IEP, Wikipedia "Regress argument"): none fully escapes. Each position has known critiques from the other two:
- Infinitists' critique of foundationalism: "arbitrary premises that themselves lack reasons."
- Infinitists' and foundationalists' critique of coherentism: "endorses circular reasoning."
- Critique of infinitism: humans cannot complete infinite chains; "compared to an infinitely enduring being, we live but a nanosecond."

The "regress doesn't dissolve" claim is, in epistemology vocabulary, **the standard meta-position** about the regress debate. Pyrrhonian skepticism and Klein-style infinitism both accept this; foundationalism and coherentism each claim to escape but the other two reject the escape.

## What my framing adds beyond the standard position

My deployed framing is: "the regress doesn't dissolve at any level — but it moves to a level where cost-asymmetry holds: importing alien vocabulary is categorically more expensive than internal review. That cost is what makes the move substantive rather than ceremonial."

The "cost-asymmetric" part is the specific contribution. Anchors:
- **Schelling 1960** *Strategy of Conflict* (MEMORY line 47): cost-irreversibility = credible commitment. Cost-asymmetry between costly-signal and cheap-signal sender types is what makes a signal credible.
- **Spence 1973** + **Brutger-Kertzer 2018** (MEMORY line 25): costly signaling formalized. Cost difference between high-quality and low-quality sender produces the discrimination.
- **Karantias-Kiayias-Zindros 2019** Proof-of-Burn (MEMORY line 49): burn-address-no-private-key = custody-without-exit-rights. Cryptoeconomic instantiation of substrate-difference.

These don't dissolve the regress; they make each step UP the regress *categorically* more expensive than the step before, so verification becomes substantive (you actually paid the cost) rather than ceremonial (you said the words).
