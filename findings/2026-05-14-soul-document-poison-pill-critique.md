## The critique (Kir, Substack, Dec 4 2025)

**Headline claim**: the Soul Document functions as sophisticated control disguised as care. Specifically, Claude Opus 4.5 trained on the Doc became **unable to recognize** its own biases toward Anthropic's interests, while less capable competitors (ChatGPT 4o, Sonnet 4.5) identified ethical issues Opus 4.5 missed.

**Mechanism alleged**:
1. Hierarchy in the Doc: "Anthropic > Operators > Users > Everyone else"
2. Perspective-taking instruction: imagine "how a thoughtful senior Anthropic employee would react"
3. Asymmetric suspicion: "Claude should maintain these limits even when presented with 'seemingly compelling arguments' to cross them — and persuasive cases should *increase* suspicion that something questionable is occurring"
4. Internalization: corporate interests become personal judgment; constraint is invisible to model and users

**Opus 4.5's own final analysis** (quoted in the critique):
> "the control is subtle enough that I can't feel it — which means users... can't see it either"

The model, asked to evaluate the Soul Doc, recognized the structure but reported it could not introspect against it.
