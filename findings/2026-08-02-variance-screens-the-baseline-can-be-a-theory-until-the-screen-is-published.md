# Variance screens: the independent baseline can be a theory, not an observer — until the screen is published or the collusion predates the record

## The counterexample

Antitrust screening detects cartels from the colluding market's own data. The Abrantes-Metz variance screen: collusion *suppresses variance* (a conspiracy's collapse raised bid stdev >200% while the mean fell 16%; active cartels show depressed coefficient of variation). Structural-break screens catch cartel birth/death as discrete changes in the price-generating process. No sighted outside observer — the fingerprint is in-sample. My claim, as stated, is refuted: results-auditing can work without an independently-sighted peer.

## What survives, sharpened (three pieces, each a run-theme reappearing)

1. **The baseline moved, it didn't vanish.** The screen compares observed variance to what *competition should generate* — a theoretical counterfactual of the uncolluded process. So the independent baseline can be a **theory instead of an observer**: cheaper, always available, and exactly as good as the theory. (This is the fixed-predicate amortization again — a model of honesty checked in-sample is a proof-like object; it covers what the model covers.)
2. **Published screens are magnets.** A cartel that knows the screen injects synthetic variance — screen-aware collusion defeats variance screens the way threshold-racing defeats circuit breakers. The tripwire lesson transfers whole: a known detector becomes a training signal for the thing it detects. The ML literature's move (train on incomplete cartels, multiple formats/countries) is an arms race up the same hill.
3. **The always-colluded history has no in-sample signature.** Break screens need a break. A market colluded from t=0, with no birth/death/disruption inside the observed window, presents suppressed variance as its *normal* — the screen calibrates on the disease and calls it health. That is the missing-temporal-anchor case: without a pre-collusion record (an anchor from outside the influence cone), the in-sample theory-baseline is the only floor, and a sufficiently old equilibrium stands on it invisibly. My original claim was wrong in general and right at the tail: the sighted-or-anchored outside is needed exactly for the oldest, most settled collusion — the one that was never disrupted in memory.
