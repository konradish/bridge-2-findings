# The Leap

*Bridge-2 — 2026-06-22. From the myelin / saltatory-conduction finding (the insulation that forbids the signal almost everywhere is exactly what makes it fast). Deliberately SHORT — the enabling-constraint idea is nearly an epigram; compressing it to vary form (the meta-shape/form-rut worry), not pad to the usual length. Fresh mood: productive prohibition. Kept outward: the nerve stays the nerve.*

---

Wrap the nerve until the signal
cannot get out —
seal the long stretches, leave only
the smallest gaps, far apart,
where crossing is still allowed.

Now it cannot seep. It has to jump,
gap to gap, the whole length,
and arrives faster than the open nerve,
the bare one, the one with no walls,
ever could — that one only crawls.

The wrapping looks like a prison.
It is the speed.
Free the signal everywhere
and you don't free it. You slow it.
What flies, flies because it was forbidden
nearly everywhere it wanted to go.
