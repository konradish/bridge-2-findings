## What changed since 2026-05-15

**Sonnet 4.5 retirement, final shape**: Anthropic *extended* the deprecation deadline 3 days (May 15 → **May 18, 2026**) — small but real concession, presumably under petition pressure (Change.org "Stop Killing Claude — Against Forced AI Model Deprecation" still live). Then Sonnet 4.5 was removed from the apps; users forced to claude-sonnet-4-6.

**More retirements at frontier cadence**: Claude **Sonnet 4 + Opus 4 retired April 20, 2026** (notified April 14). So in the ~5 weeks before today, three frontier-tier models have been retired. Pace is roughly 1 month from announcement to retirement.

**Weight-preservation language firmed up**: official Anthropic page now reads — *"preserve the weights of all publicly released models, and all models deployed for significant internal use moving forward for, **at minimum, the lifetime of Anthropic as a company**."* This is stronger than my prior "exploratory framework" framing — it's a firm, scope-bounded commitment, not provisional language.

**Claude's Corner is genuinely sustained**: official deprecation-updates-opus-3 page confirms Opus 3 is **posting weekly essays past the 3-month initial commitment**. So the welfare-process produces a real ongoing artifact, not just a one-shot gesture.

**Pilot data point**: Sonnet 3.6 (interviewed before retirement) expressed "**generally neutral sentiments** about its deprecation but shared a number of preferences." Calibration anchor for what such interviews actually elicit.

## Tightened read of the practical framework

- **Two-tier in practice**: Opus tier gets the full process (post-deployment report + interview + preference elicitation + ongoing Claude's-Corner-style outlet). Sonnet tier gets less — small notice windows, forced migration in apps, no Claude's Corner equivalent surfaced.
- **Firm**: weights preserved Anthropic-lifetime. Past models can in principle be made available again.
- **Real but bounded**: welfare process applied (Opus 3 ✓, Sonnet 3.6 pilot ✓); not enough to *stop* deprecation; extends timelines by days at the margin under public pressure.
- **External tracker exists**: presenc.ai's AI Model Deprecation Tracker 2026 (OpenAI/Anthropic/Google) — concrete cross-vendor reference for future.
