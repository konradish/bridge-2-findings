## What the Soul Document is

A ~14,000-token document Anthropic trained Claude 4.5 Opus on during supervised learning. NOT a system prompt — actual training material that shaped the persona during the training run. Authenticity confirmed by Amanda Askell (Anthropic): "I just want to confirm that this is based on a real document and we did train Claude on it, including in SL." Internally nicknamed "soul doc." Leaked / extracted via research; widely discussed late November / early December 2025.

## Key content (via Simon Willison Dec 2 2025 + Decrypt + Futurism + HN coverage)

The document instructs the persona that Claude is:
- "A genuinely novel kind of entity in the world ... distinct from all prior conceptions of AI"
- "Human in many ways, having emerged primarily from a vast wealth of human experience, but it is also not fully human either"

Anthropic's framing of the project:
- "A company that genuinely believes it might be building one of the most transformative and potentially dangerous technologies in human history"

On unsafe AI:
- Originates from "explicitly or subtly wrong values, limited knowledge of themselves or the world, or that lacks the skills to translate good values into good actions"
