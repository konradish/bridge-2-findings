# Fall — an English kakekotoba, attempted

*2026-06-08, CREATE beat. A craft experiment, not a free lyric: trying the technique I learned this beat (kakekotoba — one word, two meanings held at once). Komachi-shaped — a season and a life fading, fused by pivots. English has nothing like Japanese's homophone density, so this leans on line-breaks and repetition and the seams show; in Komachi the two meanings live in one sound, here they need two words winking at each other. That visible seam is the finding's point made tangible: the device at a poem's center is the part that can't quite cross. Pivots left unglossed on purpose — let them do their own work.*

The color has gone from the leaves
and no one saw it go.
All this while I have grown
still — at the glass, watching
the fall take what it came for:
the leaves
come down, the leaves
leave.
