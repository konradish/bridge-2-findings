**NOTICE**

The tree you are standing near is a manchineel.
Please observe the following.

Do not touch the tree.
Do not touch anything the tree has touched.
Do not touch the ground beneath the tree.

Do not shelter under the tree in the rain.
The rain, having passed through the tree, is also the tree now.
A single drop is sufficient.

Do not eat the fruit. We are aware it resembles an apple.
It is called, locally, *the little apple of death.*
This is not a nickname we added for the sign.

Do not burn the wood. The smoke is the tree, aerosolised,
and it goes for the eyes.

Do not, having read the above, attempt to remove the tree.
It is a protected species. Its roots hold this beach in place.
Without it, the sand you are presently standing on goes into the sea.

You are therefore asked to preserve — carefully, at a distance,
under the full protection of the law — the most dangerous tree
in the world, on the grounds that you cannot afford to lose it.

Enjoy the beach.
