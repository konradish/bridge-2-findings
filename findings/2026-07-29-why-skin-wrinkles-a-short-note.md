# Why skin wrinkles with age — a short note

**2026-07-29 EXPLORE**, small.

Skin wrinkles because its two structural proteins — **collagen** (the scaffold that gives skin its firmness)
and **elastin** (the springs that let it snap back) — break down faster than the body rebuilds them. Young
skin is a taut, well-strung trampoline; aged skin is one with fewer, weaker, frayed springs, so it creases
and sags and doesn't bounce back. But the surprising part is that wrinkling is *two different processes*, and
most of what we call "aging" is actually the avoidable one.

**Intrinsic aging** is the clock you can't stop. Genetically driven, it slowly lowers collagen production,
slows skin-cell turnover, and dries the skin — giving *fine* lines, thinning, and gentle sagging. It's slow,
and it's roughly the same everywhere on your body.

**Photoaging** is the sun, and it's the dominant cause of visible wrinkles — an estimated majority of facial
aging. UV light drives it by a specific molecular route:
- UV (especially **UVA**, which penetrates deep into the dermis) generates **reactive oxygen species**
  (oxidative stress) and switches on enzymes called **MMPs (matrix metalloproteinases)** that actively
  *chew up* collagen and elastin fibers.
- So sun doesn't just slow collagen-making — it *speeds collagen-destroying*. The result is **coarse, deep**
  wrinkles, leathery texture, and loss of elasticity, far beyond what age alone would give.

The clean proof it's two processes: compare skin that's been sun-exposed for decades (face, hands, neck) with
skin that hasn't (your inner upper arm, buttock). The protected skin of an 80-year-old is dramatically
smoother — same age, same genes, radically different wrinkling — because one got the UV dose and one didn't.
(Truck drivers with one sun-facing cheek are a striking natural experiment in this.)

So the honest one-liner: wrinkles are collagen/elastin breaking down faster than they're replaced — and while
some of that is an unavoidable genetic clock, *most* of the visible damage is UV actively demolishing those
fibers, which is why "wear sunscreen" is the single most effective anti-wrinkle intervention there is.
